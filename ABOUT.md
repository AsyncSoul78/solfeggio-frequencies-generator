# About the solfeggio frequencies

The solfeggio frequencies are nine tones: 174, 285, 396, 417, 528, 639, 741, 852 and 963 Hz. Six of them were linked to the old hymn syllables UT, RE, MI, FA, SOL and LA in books published between the 1970s and the 1990s. The other three came later. These days people mostly play them quietly in the background for meditation, yoga and sleep.

## Why a bare tone

Most solfeggio videos bury the tone under music, rain or a drone. [This generator](https://toolster.net/tone_generator/solfeggio-frequencies) plays only the tone itself: exactly 528 Hz, exactly 963 Hz, nothing else. So you can compare the tones honestly, put one under your own music, or just hear what a particular number sounds like.

## The most popular one

528 Hz, by far. It is the MI tone, and meditation videos often call it the love frequency. On a piano it lands between C5 and C#5, 16 cents above C5.

## The 3, 6, 9 pattern

Add up the digits of each tone until one digit is left and you get 3, 6 or 9 every time: 174 gives 1+7+4 = 12, then 1+2 = 3. That pattern is a big part of why the set is popular online. The helper script in this repository prints it next to each frequency.

## Do they work?

No reliable study shows that these exact numbers do anything special to the body. Claims about healing or repairing DNA have no scientific backing. A calm, steady sound can still help you relax, and that is an honest reason to listen. Just don't use it instead of medical care.
